Legal Basis
This certificate draft is prepared to support the procedural requirements of Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023, which provides for a certificate that identifies the device, its operator, the method by which the electronic record was produced, and its integrity as shown by hash values. Processing of the source material for the purposes of this forensic examination falls within the exemptions available to authorised law-enforcement and intelligence-gathering functions under Section 17(1)(c) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. That exemption is limited in scope: it applies only to processing by or for state agencies acting within their lawful mandate and does not constitute a general licence to process personal data.
